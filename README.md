## About

I'm George, an **AI Developer and Software Engineer (Full-Stack)** at **RENER Energy S.A.** I build Nexus, the company's **EMS and SCADA platform** for battery energy storage (BESS) and solar PV parks. I work across the backend, the web and mobile apps, and the pipelines that ship them.

Outside RENER I design, build, deploy and run websites and web platforms for businesses in Greece and Cyprus. Nine of them are live below. Most of this work lives in private repositories, so this page describes it instead.

I take features from the ticket to production: design notes and ADRs, code, tests, review, CI/CD, deployment and the on-call fix when an alarm fires at night. I'm finishing an **MSc in Human-Centered AI** at the University of Gothenburg (thesis remaining) and hold a **BSc in Software Engineering and Management** from Chalmers / University of Gothenburg.

**AI Developer · Software Engineer (Full-Stack)** · Aug 2025 to present · remote
